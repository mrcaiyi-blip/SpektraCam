# SpektraCam iOS 隐私政策 · Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective date: 2026-11-09

## 简体中文

本政策适用于 SpektraCam 的 App Store iOS 版本。

### 照片与相机权限

你授权相机访问后，SpektraCam 使用相机显示取景画面并拍照。RAW 显影在设备上完成。App 将 JPEG 和拍摄时间、胶卷、相纸等信息保存在应用相册中。SpektraCam 不会把取景画面、RAW 或成片上传到开发者的服务器。

如果你允许写入系统相册，App 会在那里另存一份 JPEG，并在设备上记录对应的照片标识，以便之后按你的选择删除系统相册副本。删除该副本时，iOS 可能要求相册读取和写入权限。系统相册的同步与备份受你的 Apple 和 iCloud 设置管理。

### 设置、共享与外部服务

语言、胶卷、相纸和拍摄设置保存在设备上。App 不要求注册账号，不含广告、分析或追踪功能。

你主动分享照片时，照片由你通过 iOS 分享菜单选定的服务处理。你主动打开政策、项目或素材来源链接时，网页由相应网站提供。第三方服务按各自的隐私政策处理数据。

### 管理权限和删除数据

你可以在 iPhone 的「设置 → App → SpektraCam」中调整相机和照片权限。撤回权限不会删除已保存的照片。你可以在应用相册删除本地照片，也可以在 Apple「照片」中删除系统相册副本。删除 App 会移除设备上的应用数据，但保留另存到系统相册的照片。

如有隐私问题或数据删除请求，请通过 [SpektraCam 项目反馈页](https://github.com/mrcaiyi-blip/SpektraCam/issues) 联系维护者。反馈是公开的，请勿附上私人照片或个人信息。

## English

This policy applies to the App Store version of SpektraCam for iOS.

### Photos and camera access

With your permission, SpektraCam uses the camera to display the viewfinder and take photos. It develops RAW captures on your device. The app stores JPEGs and details such as capture time, film stock, and print paper in its in-app gallery. SpektraCam does not upload your viewfinder feed, RAW captures, or photos to developer servers.

If you allow saving to Apple Photos, the app saves a separate JPEG copy there and stores its Photos identifier on your device so you can delete that copy later if you choose. iOS may ask for read and write access when you request deletion. System photo library syncing and backups are managed by your Apple and iCloud settings.

### Settings, sharing, and external services

Your language, film, paper, and camera preferences are stored on your device. The app does not require an account and includes no advertising, analytics, or tracking.

When you choose to share a photo, the service you select in the iOS share sheet handles it. When you open a policy, project, or source link, the corresponding website handles that visit under its own privacy policy.

### Managing permissions and deleting data

You can change camera and photo permissions under Settings → Apps → SpektraCam. Revoking permissions leaves existing photos in place. You can delete local photos from the in-app gallery and delete system copies from Apple Photos. Deleting the app removes its data from your device and leaves any copies saved to Apple Photos in your library.

For privacy questions or requests about deleting data, contact the maintainers through the [SpektraCam issue tracker](https://github.com/mrcaiyi-blip/SpektraCam/issues). Issues are public. Please omit private photos and personal information.
